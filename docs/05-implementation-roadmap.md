# 05 — Implementation Roadmap

## Purpose
The implementation roadmap translates the ISMS requirements, risk treatment decisions, and selected controls into a phased programme of implementation, assurance, and continual improvement.

The source implementation programme follows an 18-month roadmap from initial scoping through certification readiness and transition to business-as-usual governance.

---

## 18-Month Roadmap

| Phase | Months | Key Activities | Main Outputs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Foundation & Scoping** | 1–3 | Define ISMS scope, context, objectives, and governance. | ISMS scope, information security policy, asset register, risk methodology, awareness programme |
| **2. Risk Assessment & Treatment** | 4–6 | Conduct risk workshops and assess information security risks. | Risk register, risk treatment plan, draft SoA, third-party assessments |
| **3. Controls Implementation** | 7–9 | Implement priority controls and strengthen operational processes. | MFA, access reviews, data classification, incident response improvements, SoA sign-off |
| **4. Audit, Review & Remediation** | 10–12 | Test control effectiveness and address identified gaps. | Internal audit results, management review, corrective actions, gap analysis |
| **5. Certification & BAU Transition** | 13–18 | Prepare for certification activities and embed ongoing governance. | Stage 1, Stage 2, remediation activities, continual improvement, and BAU governance |

---

## Training and Assurance
Training and assurance activities support the implementation programme throughout the roadmap.

**Representative Activities:**
* ISMS awareness
* ISO 27001 fundamentals
* Secure coding
* Data classification
* Incident response tabletop exercises
* Phishing simulations
* PCI DSS and GDPR refreshers
* Internal audits
* Management reviews
